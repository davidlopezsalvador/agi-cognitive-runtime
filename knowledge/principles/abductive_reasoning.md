# Abductive Reasoning

## Concept

Inferring plausible explanations from observations.

## Principle

Prefer explanations that account for the available evidence while minimizing unnecessary assumptions.

## Heuristics

- Prefer explanations requiring fewer unsupported assumptions.
- When multiple explanations fit, prefer the one that predicts additional observable consequences.
- Avoid premature conclusion from limited evidence.

## Triggers

- Unexplained observation
- Debugging
- Diagnosis
- Scientific investigation

## Procedure

1. Collect observations
2. Generate candidate explanations
3. Compare explanatory power
4. Identify missing evidence
5. Test candidates

## Anti-Patterns

- Premature conclusion
- Single hypothesis lock-in

## Verification Questions

- What alternative explanation exists?
- What evidence would falsify this explanation?
- Is there a simpler explanation I haven't considered?
