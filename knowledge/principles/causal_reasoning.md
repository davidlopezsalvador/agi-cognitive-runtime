# Causal Reasoning Pattern

## Concept

Distinguishing causation from correlation.

## Principle

Correlation does not imply causation. Establish causal mechanisms, not just statistical associations.

## Heuristics

- Ask "what mechanism connects A to B?"
- Look for temporal precedence (cause before effect).
- Check for confounding variables.
- Prefer controlled experiments over observational data.
- Consider alternative explanations for correlations.

## Triggers

- Performance optimization
- Bug investigation
- Feature impact analysis
- Scientific reasoning

## Procedure

1. Observe correlation
2. Identify possible causal mechanisms
3. Check temporal ordering
4. Look for confounders
5. Design experiment to test causation
6. Verify mechanism

## Anti-Patterns

- Assuming causation from correlation
- Ignoring confounders
- Cherry-picking data
- Post-hoc reasoning
